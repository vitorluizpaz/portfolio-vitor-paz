About

My interests sit around combinatorics, probability, and numerical methods. I like problems where a mathematical idea has to survive contact with an implementation.

Most of my work starts small: a question, a script, a counterexample. If it holds up, it becomes a tool. This site is where I keep the results that are related to mathematics.

I also spend time on physics, mainly where computation makes a model easier to test.
